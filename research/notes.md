# Source notes — AI advisor for food trucks

- **Report date / public URL:** 2026-10-28 · https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/today
- **IdeaBrowser snapshot:** IB Index 6.5; SaaS; $2M–$4M ARR ceiling; moderate difficulty; $5K–$15K initial capital; 60–90 days to first revenue; year-one estimate $18K–$30K.
- **Buyer:** owner-operators of mobile food trucks/trailers rotating among events, farmers markets, breweries, and lots—not fixed-location trucks.
- **Painful status quo:** check weather morning and night, scan event pages, remember venue history, and make a gut call about location and prep. A wrong call destroys fresh inventory and a week’s margin.
- **Evidence:** report cites 92,257 US food-truck businesses (IBISWorld via GoFoodservice), 48.7K r/foodtrucks members, named operators reporting 40–70% sales declines, 60% slow-weather drops, and one event rainout leaving 300 pizzas of dough against a hoped-for $20K day. These are directional public receipts, not proof of willingness to pay.
- **Wedge:** an explainable spot score combining hourly weather, spot-specific fees, the truck’s own sales history, and prep cost. The demo calculates a go/pivot/skip call and prep quantity; it does not pretend to run AI.
- **Why now:** revenue growth reportedly slowed to 0.2%, margins average near 5%, weather volatility is increasing, and forecast inputs are accessible via Open-Meteo/Tomorrow.io. Owner AI adoption is still early.
- **Competitors:** Goodfynd (direct vertical threat; 500+ trucks; FyndAI events finder $25/mo), Lineup.ai ($79–$149/location restaurant forecasting), Roaming Hunger, Best Food Trucks, Square, Toast, Truckster, VendorLoop, StreetFoodFinder, and DIY guides/spreadsheets.
- **Demand/distribution:** hand-sell in r/foodtrucks, regional Facebook groups, state/metro associations, and lot operators. Search demand is weak (report: 10/mo “food truck management software,” 30/mo “food truck software”).
- **Offer ladder:** free Prep Waste Calculator → $19 one-time Spot Scorecard → $29/mo 6am Daily Brief by text → $79/mo Prep Sheet + Pivot Nudge.
- **Economics from report:** assumptions of 150 calculator signups/mo, 15% to scorecard, 40% to brief, 85% monthly retention, yielding roughly 60 core subscribers and ~$2,340 total MRR at month 12. Four of five rows remain assumptions.
- **Largest risks:** Goodfynd can copy the brief; sparse per-spot history delays value; category education and hand sales are required; SMS churn may arrive before models learn; no observed paid demand yet.
- **Cited source domains:** CNBC, CBC News, CBS News Chicago, Spectrum News, FLIP Food Truck Statistics, Climate Central, Open-Meteo, National Restaurant Association/Homebase, IBISWorld/GoFoodservice, YouTube, Reddit, and competitor sites. Verify primary claims before commercial use.
