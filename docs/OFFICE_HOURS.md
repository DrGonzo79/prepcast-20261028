# Office Hours diagnostic

**Mode:** startup, pre-product. **Position:** do not build an “AI advisor platform.” Sell a manual morning decision first.

## Six forcing answers

1. **Demand reality:** operators publicly describe catastrophic weather/prep losses, but nobody has paid for this product. Pain evidence is strong; demand evidence is absent.
2. **Status quo:** two weather checks, event/lot messages, fee terms in texts or notes, remembered sales, spreadsheet math, then gut feel. Cost is unsold perishables, staff time, venue fees, and forgone revenue.
3. **Desperate user:** a rotating owner-operator with one truck, 3–8 recurring spots, thin cash reserves, and perishables prepared before demand is known. A bad Saturday can erase the week.
4. **Narrowest paid wedge:** before 6am, deliver one auditable go/pivot/skip recommendation plus prep units for tomorrow’s scheduled spot. Charge only after it prevents one measurable loss.
5. **Observation plan:** shadow five owners through 10 morning decisions without helping. Record inputs consulted, decision latency, changes, actual gross, waste, and whether the explanation is trusted. Surveys and guided demos do not count.
6. **Future-fit:** more erratic weather and cheaper hyperlocal data increase value, but POS vendors can absorb the feature. Defensibility must come from proprietary truck/spot outcome history and a distribution relationship, not generic AI prose.

## Premises challenged

- Weather alone is not the product; the paid decision is weather × spot economics × own history.
- An SMS subscription is premature before five operators repeatedly follow the call.
- The direct competitor is the operator’s gut and notes first, Goodfynd second.

## Approaches considered

### A — Manual morning concierge (recommended)
Effort S · Risk low. Founder produces briefs from operator inputs. Fastest route to behavior and payment evidence; not scalable.

### B — Explainable spot scorecard
Effort M · Risk medium. Self-serve calculator with visible fee, weather, history, and prep math. Reusable product core; requires disciplined data entry.

### C — POS-integrated forecasting system
Effort XL · Risk high. Automated ingestion, weather/events, forecasts, SMS, and learning loops. Strong ideal architecture; premature before demand and partner access.

**Decision:** ship B as a transparent demo, while validating with A. Do not implement C yet.

## Success criteria and assignment

Within 14 days: observe 50 real decisions across five trucks; obtain at least three explicit YES responses to $29/mo after a followed recommendation; record one avoided waste event worth >$100. The next action is to recruit five rotating trucks and run the brief manually for two weeks.
